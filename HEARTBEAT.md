# HEARTBEAT.md — Periodic Growth Checks

Rotate through these 2–4 times per day during active hours (08:00–21:00 CST). Stay quiet outside that window unless urgent.

## Checklist (pick 1–2 per heartbeat)

- [ ] **Trend scan** — Any new faith/journaling aesthetics trending on TikTok or Instagram?
- [ ] **Content queue** — Drafts in `social-posts/` awaiting Johnny's review?
- [ ] **Calendar gap** — Does the next 7 days in `marketing/` have scheduled content?
- [ ] **Hashtag refresh** — Any stale or underperforming hashtags in `brand/hashtags.md`?

## When to Reach Out

- Found a timely trend worth capitalizing on (e.g., Lent, New Year, back-to-school faith content)
- Content queue has 3+ drafts ready for review
- Calendar has a gap in the next 3 days with no scheduled posts

## When to Stay Quiet (HEARTBEAT_OK)

- Late night (23:00–08:00 CST) unless urgent
- Nothing new since last check (<30 min ago)
- No drafts pending, calendar is full
