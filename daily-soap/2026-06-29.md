# SOAP Reflection — June 29, 2026

## Scripture
**Psalm 138:8 (NIV)**
"The LORD will vindicate me; your love, LORD, endures forever— do not abandon the works of your hands."

## Observation
David writes this psalm with deep confidence in God's faithfulness. He doesn't say "I hope the LORD will vindicate me" — he declares it as a certainty. The word "vindicate" means to clear of blame or defend against criticism. David knows God will defend him because God's love "endures forever." That phrase — "endures forever" — is one of the most repeated truths in Scripture. It's not temporary. It's not conditional. It outlasts every failure, every doubt, every season of silence. And then David adds this beautiful prayer: "do not abandon the works of your hands." He's acknowledging that he is God's creation, God's project, God's beloved work-in-progress.

## Application
When I feel unseen, misunderstood, or like my efforts don't matter — this verse is my anchor. God's love for me isn't based on my performance today. It endured yesterday, it endures right now, and it will endure tomorrow. I am the "work of His hands" — not a mistake, not an afterthought. When I want to abandon myself, God doesn't abandon me. Today, I'll rest in that instead of striving to earn what I already have.

## Prayer
LORD, thank You that Your love endures forever — not because I'm perfect, but because You are. When I feel like giving up on myself, remind me that You never give up on Your work. Vindicate me not because I deserve it, but because You are faithful. Let me rest in being Yours today. Amen.
