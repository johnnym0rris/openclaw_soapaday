# SOAP Reflection — Deuteronomy 6:5

## Verse
**Deuteronomy 6:5 (NIV)**
"Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your strength."

## Observation
Moses delivers this command to Israel as the centerpiece of the Shema — the foundational confession of Jewish faith. Notice the totality: "all your heart" (emotions and desires), "all your soul" (inner being and will), and "all your strength" (energy and resources). God isn't asking for partial devotion or compartmentalized faith. He wants every dimension of our existence oriented toward Him. This isn't a feeling-based love; it's a covenant commitment that encompasses our whole person.

## Application
Where am I holding back from God? Which areas of my life are still "partially mine" — my finances, my schedule, my ambitions, my relationships? This week, I'll do a practical audit: pick one area I've been reluctant to surrender fully and intentionally offer it to God. Maybe it's waking up 15 minutes earlier for prayer, or tithing faithfully, or releasing a grudge. Wholehearted love starts with one surrendered step.

## Prayer
Lord, You know how easily I divide my affections — giving You part of me while keeping the rest for myself. Today I confess the areas where I've been half-hearted. Teach me what it means to love You with ALL of me — my emotions, my will, my energy, my resources. Take what's Yours. Make me wholly Yours. In Jesus' name, amen.
