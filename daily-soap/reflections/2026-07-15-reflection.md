# SOAP Reflection — July 15, 2026

## Scripture
**James 1:21 (NIV)**
"Therefore, get rid of all moral filth and the evil that is so prevalent and humbly accept the word planted in you, which can save you."

---

## Observation
James writes to believers scattered across the Roman world, urging them to move beyond passive listening into active transformation. The phrase "get rid of all moral filth" uses strong language — it's not gentle spring cleaning, but a deliberate stripping away of what corrupts. James connects this removal with humble acceptance of God's word, suggesting that making space (removing filth) and receiving truth (accepting the word) are two sides of the same coin. The word "planted" implies the seed of Scripture is already in us through hearing — our job is to cultivate it, not just possess it.

## Application
Where am I allowing "moral filth" to linger in my life? It might not be dramatic sin — perhaps it's cynicism, gossip, comparison, or apathy that I've normalized. James says to "get rid of it" — actively, intentionally. Then, with that space cleared, humbly accept what God is planting. This week, I can practice this by identifying one area where I've become numb to compromise and replacing it with intentional Scripture meditation.

## Prayer
Lord, show me what I've tolerated that You want removed. Give me the courage to strip away what hinders my growth and the humility to receive Your word with open hands. Let the truth You've planted in me take deep root and bear fruit that lasts. In Jesus' name, amen.
