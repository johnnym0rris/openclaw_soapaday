# SOAP Reflection — June 29, 2026

## Scripture
**Psalm 4:8 (NIV)**
"In peace I will lie down and sleep, for you alone, Lord, make me dwell in safety."

## Observation
David writes this from a place of complete trust. He doesn't say "I hope I can sleep" or "maybe I'll find peace." He declares it with certainty: "In peace I WILL lie down and sleep." His ability to rest isn't tied to his circumstances — it's tied to his God. The word "alone" is key. David recognizes that true safety doesn't come from locks, armies, or status. It comes from the Lord alone. This is a radical peace that persists even when chaos surrounds him.

## Application
How often do I lie down with my mind racing, replaying worries, planning tomorrow's battles? David's example invites me to transfer my trust from my own problem-solving to God's sovereign care. Tonight, when I close my eyes, I want to practice this intentional handover — "You alone, Lord." Not my plans, not my backup plans, not my anxiety. Just You. That's where real rest lives.

## Prayer
Lord, teach me to lie down in peace. When my mind wants to rehearse every worry, remind me that You alone are my safety. I transfer every concern into Your hands tonight. Guard my sleep, guard my heart, and help me wake trusting You still. In Jesus' name, Amen.
