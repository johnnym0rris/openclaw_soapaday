# SOAPaDay Brand Voice Guide

See the canonical brand voice at `brand/voice.md` in the workspace root.

This file provides additional detail for the b2c-marketing skill.

## Quick Reference

**We are:** A warm invitation into daily Bible study
**We sound:** Reflective, encouraging, authentic, respectful
**We're for:** People who want consistency in their faith journey
**We're against:** Clickbait, preachiness, fake testimonials, proof-texting
**We believe:** Small daily steps build lasting habits

**When stuck, ask:** "What would a thoughtful friend who journals their faith say?"

## Content Pillars

1. **Habit Formation** — Building a daily rhythm, starting small, grace over guilt
2. **SOAP Framework** — Teaching the method through examples and prompts
3. **Aesthetic Inspiration** — Beautiful journaling, morning routines, creative expression
4. **Community** — Shared faith journeys, encouragement, belonging

## Competitor Positioning

**YouVersion / Bible apps:** We're focused on reflection and journaling, not just reading.
**Physical journals:** We're digital, portable, and guided by the SOAP framework.
**Generic wellness apps:** We're faith-grounded, not secular mindfulness.
