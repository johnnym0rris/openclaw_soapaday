# B2C Marketing — SOAPaDay

## Core Principles

- **Lead with the person's desire**, not the app. Speak to the longing for consistency, reflection, and connection with scripture.
- **One message per piece.** Don't cram every feature into one post.
- **Front-load value.** Hook attention in the first 1–2 lines.
- **Match platform tone.** Instagram ≠ TikTok ≠ Pinterest.
- **CTA clarity.** Every piece needs a clear, low-friction next step.
- **Theological respect.** Never use scripture manipulatively. See `brand/voice.md`.

## Platform Guidelines

### Instagram (Primary)
- Visual-first. Caption supports the image/video.
- Short, warm captions with emojis.
- Reels: hook in 0–3 seconds, aesthetic journaling vibes.
- Best times: Tue–Thu, 7–9am or 7–9pm CST.

### TikTok
- Trend-aware but authentic — don't force faith into unrelated trends.
- Text overlays, "day in my life" Bible study formats.
- 15–60 second hooks, conversational tone.

### Pinterest
- Beautiful verse graphics, journaling templates.
- Keyword-rich descriptions for SEO.
- Vertical pins (2:3 ratio), warm aesthetic.

## Content Frameworks

### PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)
1. State the problem (inconsistent Bible study, don't know where to start)
2. Agitate — why it matters spiritually and emotionally
3. Present SOAPaDay as the simple path forward

### Story-Driven
1. Open with a relatable moment (morning rush, guilt about skipping devotions)
2. Show the journey toward consistency
3. Deliver the lesson — small steps, daily rhythm
4. Tie to SOAPaDay naturally

## Workflow

### For Social Media Posts
1. Identify platform and goal (awareness, engagement, app download)
2. Choose a framework
3. Draft 2–3 headline hooks
4. Write body copy (follow platform guidelines)
5. Add CTA and hashtags from `brand/hashtags.md`
6. Suggest image/video direction (see social-image-generator skill)

### For Devotional Content (/scripture mode)
1. Select scripture passage (accurate reference)
2. Write SOAP prompt: Scripture → Observation → Application → Prayer
3. Keep accessible for beginners
4. Save to `social-posts/` or `marketing/`

## Review Checklist

Before approving any marketing copy:
- [ ] Hook grabs attention in 3 seconds?
- [ ] Pain point or aspiration is clear?
- [ ] One clear message (not feature-dumping)?
- [ ] CTA is specific and low-friction?
- [ ] Tone matches platform and brand voice?
- [ ] Scripture references are accurate?
- [ ] No fabricated testimonials or statistics?
- [ ] Spelling and grammar checked?
