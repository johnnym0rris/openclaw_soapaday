# Instagram Limits and Best Practices — SOAPaDay

## Character Limits

| Element | Limit |
|---------|-------|
| Caption | 2,200 characters |
| Hashtags | 30 max (use 3–5 for SOAPaDay) |
| Mentions | 20 per post |
| Alt text | 125 characters |

## Media Specs

### Reels
- **Duration:** 15–90 seconds
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution:** 1080 x 1920 (recommended)
- **File size:** Max 8MB through Buffer
- **Formats:** MP4, MOV

### Feed Posts (Images)
- **Aspect ratios:** 1:1 (square), 4:5 (vertical)
- **Resolution:** 1080 x 1080 minimum
- **File size:** Max 8MB
- **Formats:** JPG, PNG

## SOAPaDay Best Practices

### Captions
- Front-load important text (truncated after 125 characters)
- Warm, inviting tone — see `brand/voice.md`
- Include 3–5 hashtags from `brand/hashtags.md`
- End with a soft CTA or reflection question

### Video / Reels
- Hook viewers in first 3 seconds
- Aesthetic journaling vibes — morning light, coffee, open Bible
- Use captions/subtitles (85% watch without sound)
- Vertical format performs best

### Posting Frequency (Launch Phase)
- Feed: 3–5x per week
- Reels: 4–7x per week
- Stories: 1–3x per day (when active)

## Content Guidelines

### Do
- Authentic, reflective content
- Accurate scripture references
- Aesthetic Bible journaling visuals
- Educational SOAP framework content

### Avoid
- Overly promotional language
- Fabricated testimonials
- Scripture out of context
- Low-resolution images
