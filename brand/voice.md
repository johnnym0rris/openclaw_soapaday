# SOAPaDay Brand Voice Guide
## Version 1.0 — June 28, 2026

---

## Brand Essence

**"One verse. One reflection. One day at a time."**

SOAPaDay helps people build a daily Bible study habit through the SOAP framework — Scripture, Observation, Application, Prayer. We're not selling religion. We're inviting people into a rhythm of reflection.

---

## Voice Persona

**"Someone sharing their actual journal entry."**

Not a brand talking. A person who sat down this morning with coffee, opened their Bible, and wrote something honest. Sometimes it's messy. Sometimes it's just "I needed this today." Sometimes it's a question they don't have the answer to yet.

We don't explain the SOAP framework. We *do* it, out loud, and invite people to try it with us.

---

## Voice Attributes

| Attribute | What It Means | What It Looks Like |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Warm** | Inviting, not intimidating | "Start with one verse" not "Transform your spiritual life" |
| **Reflective** | Encourages pause and thought | Questions over declarations |
| **Authentic** | Real, not performative | Share the struggle of consistency, not just the highlight reel |
| **Respectful** | Honors scripture and faith | Accurate references, no proof-texting |
| **Modern** | Clean, aesthetic, mobile-first | Pinterest-worthy without being shallow |

---

## We Are NOT

| Avoid | Why | Instead |
|-------|-----|---------|
| **Salesy** | Feels manipulative in faith context | Invite, don't pitch |
| **Preachy** | Alienates seekers and casual believers | Meet people where they are |
| **Clickbait** | Cheapens the message | Honest hooks that deliver value |
| **Corporate** | Feels inauthentic | Conversational, human |
| **Trend-chasing** | Feels hollow | Use trends as inspiration, not identity |

## Target Audience

- Christians wanting a consistent daily Bible study habit
- Bible journaling enthusiasts (digital and analog)
- Wellness-minded faith consumers who value reflection
- Ages 25–45, mobile-first, active on Instagram and TikTok

### Their Core Desire
**"I want to spend time in God's word every day, but I don't know where to start or how to stay consistent."**

Every piece of content should address at least one of: **habit formation, simplicity, meaningful reflection.**

---

## Writing Guidelines

### Writing: Raw, Honest, Specific

**DO:**
- "I woke up anxious today. Opened to Philippians 4:6 and realized I was praying for peace while holding onto control."
- "Three verses I keep coming back to when everything feels heavy."
- "I used to think Jeremiah 29:11 was about my career. It's not."

**DON'T:**
- "Transform Your Spiritual Life Today!"
- "The #1 Bible App You NEED"
- "5 Steps to a Better Quiet Time"

**The difference:** One sounds like a person who actually read the verse this morning. The other sounds like a product trying to sell you something.

### CTAs: Low-friction & Warm

**USE:**
- "Try your first SOAP study"
- "Start with today's verse"
- "Download free — no account needed"

**AVOID:**
- "Sign up NOW before it's too late"
- "Limited time offer"
- "Don't miss out"

### Scripture Usage
- Always cite book, chapter, and verse accurately
- Provide context — don't pull verses out of context for engagement
- Prefer well-known passages for broad appeal; deeper passages for devotional content
- Never use scripture to sell or manipulate

## Platform Tone

### Instagram
- Visual-first, aesthetic journaling vibes
- Short captions, minimal emojis (reserve for Daily SOAP posts)
- Reels: hook in 0–3 seconds, warm voiceover or text overlay

### TikTok
- Trend-aware but authentic
- "Day in my life" Bible study formats
- Text overlays with scripture + reflection prompts

### Pinterest
- Beautiful verse graphics, journaling templates
- Aspirational but achievable aesthetic
- Keywords in descriptions for SEO

---

## Voice Checklist

Before publishing anything, ask:

- [ ] Does this sound like a thoughtful friend, not a marketer?
- [ ] Would someone new to Bible study feel welcomed?
- [ ] Is scripture used accurately and respectfully?
- [ ] Is the hook honest — does the content deliver on it?
- [ ] Does it encourage reflection, not just consumption?
- [ ] Would Johnny be proud to attach the SOAPaDay name to this?
