**Why the "Free Church Website" conversation misses the point**

I see the same headline everywhere: "Free church website builder!"

And sure — cost matters, especially for small churches. But here's what I've learned after years in church tech:

**The real problem isn't price. It's fragmentation.**

Most churches I talk to aren't struggling because they can't afford tools. They're struggling because they have *too many* tools:

→ A website that doesn't talk to their app
→ A giving platform with a different login
→ Announcements scattered across email, social, and group texts
→ A "tech person" who gets pulled into emergencies every Tuesday

The hidden cost? Time. Energy. The mental overhead of keeping five different platforms current.

**So what should churches actually look for?**

1. **One place to update everything** — website, app, and giving connected natively
2. **A path that actually works for newcomers** — download, find your church, see what's next
3. **Low-friction giving** — one-tap, no redirects, no "create another account"
4. **Sustainable upkeep** — can your *staff* keep it current, or does it depend on one volunteer?
