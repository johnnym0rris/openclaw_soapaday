Let's talk about the question every pastor asks but nobody wants to answer first:

**"How much is this actually going to cost me?"**

→ Setup fee: $299
→ Custom domain: $15/month
→ Giving integration: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
→ App publishing: "Contact sales"
→ Support: Extra

By month three, that "free" tool costs more than your coffee budget. And you still have five logins.

**Here's our pricing. No asterisks.**

Full app. Full website. Full giving. No setup fees. No transaction fees. We mean it.

✅ **Everyone else:** One flat monthly price.
Unlimited members. Unlimited push notifications. Unlimited updates. One login.

✅ **What's included:**
→ Branded church app (iOS + Android)
→ Connected website
→ Mobile giving (no per-transaction fees)
→ Push notifications
→ Small groups & community
→ Sermon notes & daily readings
→ Real human support

**What's not included:**
→ Setup fees
→ Hidden costs
→ "Contact sales" surprises

We built myChelper because we were tired of watching churches get nickel-and-dimed for tools that should just work.

Ministry is already complex enough. Your tech budget shouldn't be.
