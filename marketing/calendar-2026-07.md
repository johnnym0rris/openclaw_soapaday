## Hook Post Templates

### "Read this if..." (4 images)
1. **Hook card** — Bold statement ("Read this if you're anxious")
2. **Verse card** — Scripture with reference
3. **Reflection card** — Short observation (2-3 sentences)
4. **CTA card** — "Start journaling today" + SOAPaDay.com

### "3 verses when..." (5 images)
1. **Hook card** — "3 verses when you feel overwhelmed"
2. **Verse 1** — First scripture
3. **Verse 2** — Second scripture
4. **Verse 3** — Third scripture
5. **CTA card** — "Study these in SOAPaDay" + URL

### "A prayer for..." (3 images)
1. **Hook card** — "A prayer for today"
2. **Prayer card** — Written prayer (4-6 sentences)
3. **CTA card** — "Write your own prayers in SOAPaDay" + URL

### "What this verse actually means" (4 images)
1. **Hook card** — "What [verse] actually means"
2. **Verse card** — Full scripture
3. **VS card** — "What people think" vs "What it actually means"
4. **CTA card** — "Study with context in SOAPaDay" + URL
