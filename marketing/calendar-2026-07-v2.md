## New Voice: Personal, Raw, Honest

### "Read this if..." (4 images)
1. **Personal hook** — "I woke up anxious today. Opened to Philippians 4:6 and realized I was praying for peace while holding onto control."
2. **Verse card** — Scripture with reference
3. **My reflection** — What I actually wrote in my journal (messy, honest, specific)
4. **Invitation** — "What's your go-to verse when anxiety hits?" or "Try journaling this today"

### "3 verses when..." (5 images)
1. **Personal hook** — "Three verses I keep coming back to when everything feels heavy"
2. **Verse 1** — Why this one matters to me
3. **Verse 2** — The context that changed everything
4. **Verse 3** — The one I have memorized
5. **Invitation** — "Which verse do you return to?"

### "A prayer for..." (3 images)
1. **Personal hook** — "I wrote this prayer last Tuesday when I felt stuck"
2. **Prayer card** — Actual written prayer (not polished, just honest)
3. **Invitation** — "Write your own. It doesn't have to sound holy."

### "I used to think..." (4 images)
1. **Confession** — "I had this on a coffee mug for years before I read the chapter"
2. **Verse card** — Full scripture
3. **What I got wrong** — My misunderstanding vs. what it actually means
4. **Invitation** — "What verse have you misunderstood?"

## Daily SOAP Template (4 images)

4. **P — Prayer** — "This is what I actually prayed (not what I think I should pray)"
